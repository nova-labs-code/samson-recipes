Recipe Submission Guidelines

All recipe submissions must adhere to the following standards to ensure clarity, safety, and usability:

  • Include the number of servings.
  • Specify preparation time, cook time, chill time (if applicable), and total time.
  • Provide a clear and accurate ingredient list with precise amounts.
  • Write step-by-step instructions that are easy to follow.
  • Ensure content is safe, family-friendly, and suitable for all audiences.

Submissions are reviewed before publication to ensure compliance. Non-compliant content may be rejected or returned for revision.
