Monster Junk Feast Bake

From Scratch | ~8–10 Servings | 20–30 min Baking | Chaotic Comfort Food

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Serving Suggestions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 1–1.5 hours
  • Cook Time: 20–30 minutes
  • Total Time: ~1 hr 45 min

🛒 Ingredients

Protein

  • 3 lb chicken breast (~6 medium, cut into chunks)
  • 2 lb ground beef (~4 patties, crumbled)
  • 1 lb bacon (~16 slices)
  • 6 oz pepperoni (~30 slices)

Dairy & Eggs

  • 8 oz cheddar (~2 cups shredded)
  • 16 oz mozzarella (~4 cups shredded + sticks)
  • 1 gallon milk (use ~5 cups total)
  • 3–4 eggs (for breading/frying)
  • ½ lb butter (~4 tbsp used)
  • 8 oz cream cheese (for jalapeño poppers)

Pasta & Grains

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni (~4 cups cooked)
  • 3 cups bread flour (pizza dough)
  • ¼ - ½ cup all-purpose flour (for tots & breading)

Vegetables & Misc

  • 5 medium russet potatoes (~2 lb, for tater tots)
  • 6–7 jalapeños (~6 oz)
  • 1 cup pickle slices
  • ¼ cup honey or syrup

Coating & Frying

  • 3 cups breadcrumbs
  • ~2 cups cooking oil

Seasonings & Extras

  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Paprika
  • Garlic powder
  • Cayenne

🍳 Instructions

Spicy Chicken Nuggets:

  1. Cut chicken into 1.5–2" chunks, season with salt, paprika, garlic powder, and cayenne.
  2. Coat each piece in flour, dip in beaten eggs, then breadcrumbs.
  3. Fry or bake until golden and crispy.

Tater Tots:

  1. Peel potatoes, boil until tender, mash lightly.
  2. Form into tots, coat lightly with flour and breadcrumbs.
  3. Fry or bake until crispy and golden.

Mac & Cheese:

  1. Cook macaroni until al dente.
  2. Melt butter, whisk in flour, gradually add milk until thickened.
  3. Stir in cheddar and mozzarella until creamy.
  4. Combine pasta with sauce.

Bacon & Pepperoni:

  1. Cook bacon until crispy, crumble.
  2. Slice pepperoni and crisp lightly in a skillet.
  3. Layer over mac & cheese and tater tots.

Jalapeño Poppers:

  1. Halve jalapeños, remove seeds, mix cream cheese with paprika & garlic powder.
  2. Fill jalapeños, coat with breadcrumbs, bake or fry until golden.

Assemble Feast:

  1. Layer mac & cheese, chicken nuggets, tater tots, bacon, pepperoni, and jalapeño poppers in a large casserole dish.
  2. Drizzle with honey or syrup.
  3. Bake at 350°F for 15–20 min until everything is warmed through.
  4. Serve immediately with optional pickle slices.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

  • Add extra shredded cheese on top for gooeyness.
  • Serve with sour cream, salsa, or dipping sauces.
  • Pair with soft rolls or biscuits for a hearty meal.
  • Top with cinnamon toast crunch for fun crunch (optional).

🗃️ Storage

  • Refrigerate leftovers in airtight container for up to 4 days.
  • Freeze individual portions for up to 2 months.
  • Reheat in oven at 325°F or microwave until hot.
