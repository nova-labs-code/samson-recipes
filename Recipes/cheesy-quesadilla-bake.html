Cheesy Veggie Quesadilla Bake

Medium | 12 Servings | Total Time: 50 Minutes

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 15 Minutes
  • Cook Time: 35 Minutes
  • Total Time: ~50 Minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • ½ cup diced red onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ¼ tsp chili powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Sauté bell peppers, onion, and garlic until softened.
  3. Stir in corn, black beans, and seasonings.
  4. Lightly grease a baking dish.
  5. Layer tortillas, veggie mixture, and cheeses.
  6. Cover and bake for 20 minutes.
  7. Uncover and bake until cheese is bubbly and lightly golden.
  8. Let cool slightly before slicing.

🗃️ Storage

  • Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
  • Reheat in the oven at 350°F or in the microwave.
