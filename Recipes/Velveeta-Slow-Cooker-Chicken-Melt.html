Velveeta™ Slow-Cooker Chicken Melt

From Frozen | 5 lbs | 5–6 Hours on HIGH | Makes 10–12 Servings

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Serving Suggestions

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 5–10 minutes
  • Cook Time: 5–6 hours on HIGH
  • Melt Time: 10–15 minutes on LOW or WARM
  • Total Time: ~5 hr 15 min to 6 hr 15 min

🛒 Ingredients

Chicken:

  • 5 lbs frozen boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup chicken broth or water
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp paprika (optional)
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning or ranch seasoning

Cheese Sauce:

  • 8 oz Velveeta™ (½ block), cubed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2–3 tbsp milk
  • 2 cups + 2 tbsp all-purpose flour (if watery add more)

🍳 Instructions

  1. Place frozen chicken into crockpot (some overlap is okay).
  2. Sprinkle all dry seasonings evenly over chicken.
  3. Pour broth around chicken (not directly on top of seasoning).
  4. Cover and cook on HIGH for 5 to 6 hours.
  5. Ensure internal temp reaches 165°F minimum.
  6. Once cooked, shred chicken with forks directly in crockpot.
  7. In a medium bowl, whisk 2 cups + 2 tbsp flour into 2–3 tbsp milk until smooth.
  8. Add Velveeta™ cubes and butter. Melt until smooth.
  9. Pour cheese sauce into shredded chicken, mix well.
  10. Cook on LOW or WARM for 10–15 minutes until sauce is thick.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

  • Serve over baked potatoes
  • Add steamed broccoli
  • Great with toasted bread or inside a sandwich
