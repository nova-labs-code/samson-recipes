Cinnamon Banana Chocolate Swirl Cheesecake

Intermediate | Servings: 10 | Total Time: 2 hr 30 min

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Serving Suggestions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep: 30 min
  • Cook: 1 hr 15 min
  • Chill: 1 hr (or overnight)
  • Total: 2 hr 30 min

🛒 Ingredients

Crust

  • 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted

Filling

  • 24 oz (680 g) cream cheese, softened
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • ½ cup sour cream

Chocolate Swirl

  • ½ cup semisweet chocolate chips, melted

Topping (optional)

  • Whipped cream
  • Sliced bananas
  • Grated chocolate or chocolate shavings

🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (160°C). Grease a 9-inch springform pan and set aside.
  2. Make crust: Combine graham cracker crumbs, sugar, and cinnamon. Stir in melted butter until mixture holds together. Press evenly into the bottom of the pan. Bake 8–10 min, then let cool.
  3. Prepare filling: Beat cream cheese until smooth. Add sugar and cinnamon, then eggs one at a time. Mix in vanilla, mashed bananas, and sour cream until creamy.
  4. Add chocolate swirl: Pour half the filling into the crust. Drizzle half the melted chocolate on top. Pour remaining filling and drizzle remaining chocolate. Swirl gently with a knife or skewer.
  5. Bake cheesecake: Bake 55–65 min until center is almost set but slightly jiggly. Turn off oven, crack door, and let cheesecake cool inside 1 hr.
  6. Chill: Cool completely, then refrigerate at least 1 hr (overnight is best).
  7. Serve: Top with whipped cream, banana slices, and chocolate shavings if desired. Slice and enjoy!

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

  • Optional: Add extra whipped cream and chocolate drizzle
  • Pair with coffee or a sweet dessert wine
  • Top with fresh banana slices for presentation

🗃️ Storage

  • Refrigerate covered for up to 4 days
  • Freeze uncut slices for up to 2 months
  • Thaw in fridge before serving
