Boneless Wings

Medium | 12 Servings | Total Time: 45 Minutes

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 15 Minutes
  • Cook Time: 30 Minutes
  • Total Time: ~45 Minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 lbs boneless chicken breast or tenders
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cornstarch
  • 2 cups breadcrumbs or panko
  • 2–3 cups vegetable oil for frying
  • ½ cup hot sauce or wing sauce
  • 2 tbsp melted butter (optional, for sauce)

🍳 Instructions

  1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces if not using tenders.
  2. Marinate chicken in buttermilk, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper for at least 10 minutes.
  3. Mix flour, cornstarch, and breadcrumbs in a shallow bowl.
  4. Dredge marinated chicken in flour mixture, pressing lightly to coat.
  5. Heat oil in a deep skillet or fryer to 350°F (175°C).
  6. Fry chicken in batches until golden brown and cooked through, ~5–6 minutes per batch. Drain on paper towels.
  7. For sauce: Mix hot sauce with melted butter, toss cooked wings until coated.
  8. Serve immediately with ranch or blue cheese dipping sauce.

🗃️ Storage

  • Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat in oven at 350°F until hot and crispy.
  • Do not freeze after tossing in sauce; freeze raw fried chicken if desired.
