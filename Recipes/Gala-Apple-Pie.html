Gala Apple Pie

Pan: 9-inch pie dish | Medium | Makes 6–8 Servings

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Serving Suggestions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 30 Minutes
  • Cook Time: 2 Hours
  • Chill Time: 50–60 Minutes
  • Total Time: ~3 hours 30 Minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 5 Gala apples, peeled and sliced
  • Granulated sugar & brown sugar
  • Flour, cinnamon, nutmeg
  • Lemon juice & vanilla
  • Butter
  • Double pie crust

🍳 Instructions

  1. Prepare and chill pie dough.
  2. Mix apple filling and let rest.
  3. Assemble pie in dish.
  4. Bake until golden and bubbling.
  5. Cool completely before slicing.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

  • Serve with vanilla ice cream
  • Add caramel drizzle
  • Pair with coffee or tea

🗃️ Storage

  • Room temperature: up to 2 days
  • Refrigerator: up to 4 days
  • Freezer: up to 2 months
