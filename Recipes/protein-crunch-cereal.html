Protein Crunch Cereal

Easy | 12 Servings | Total Time: ~25 Minutes

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Serving Suggestions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 10 Minutes
  • Cook Time: 15 Minutes
  • Total Time: ~25 Minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 3 cups rolled oats
  • 1 cup protein powder (any flavor)
  • ½ cup almond butter or peanut butter
  • ¼ cup honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ½ tsp cinnamon (optional)
  • Pinch of salt
  • ½ cup chopped nuts or seeds (optional)
  • ¼ cup dark chocolate chips (optional)

🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
  2. Mix oats, protein powder, cinnamon, salt, and nuts/seeds in a large bowl.
  3. Warm almond/peanut butter and honey in a small saucepan until smooth; stir in vanilla.
  4. Pour wet mixture over dry ingredients and mix until coated.
  5. Spread evenly on a lined baking sheet and press down gently.
  6. Bake 12–15 minutes, stirring halfway, until golden and crisp.
  7. Let cool completely, then break into clusters; add chocolate chips if desired.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

  • Serve with milk or plant-based alternative.
  • Sprinkle over yogurt or smoothie bowls for extra crunch.

🗃️ Storage

  • Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 2 weeks.
  • Refrigerate for up to 1 month for longer freshness.
  • Freeze in a sealed container for up to 3 months; thaw at room temperature before serving.
