Cheesy Sausage Casserole

Medium | Comfort Food | Makes 12 Servings

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 20 Minutes
  • Cook Time: 50–60 Minutes
  • Total Time: 70–80 Minutes

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1½ lbs breakfast sausage
  • 9 large eggs
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sour cream or Greek yogurt (optional)
  • 9 slices bread, cubed or 5 cups hash browns
  • 2 tbsp melted butter or bacon grease
  • 2½ cups shredded cheese, divided
  • ¾ cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp melted butter (topping)
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika

🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9×13-inch dish.
  2. Brown sausage and drain grease.
  3. Whisk eggs, water, mayo, sour cream, and seasoning.
  4. Toss bread or hash browns with melted butter.
  5. Mix sausage, egg mixture, bread, and half the cheese.
  6. Spread evenly into dish.
  7. Top with remaining cheese and buttered breadcrumbs.
  8. Bake uncovered 50–60 minutes until set and golden.
  9. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.

🗃️ Storage

  • Refrigerate leftovers up to 4 days
  • Freeze slices up to 2 months
  • Reheat at 325°F or microwave
