Taco Mac N Cheese Bake

Mild | Uses Biscuit Base | Makes 6–8 Servings

Time Breakdown Ingredients Instructions Serving Suggestions Storage

🕒 Time Breakdown

  • Prep Time: 20 minutes
  • Cook Time: 30 minutes
  • Total Time: 50 minutes

🛒 Ingredients

Main:

  • 1 can refrigerated biscuits (8 biscuits)
  • 2 cups cooked macaroni
  • 1 lb ground turkey or beef
  • 1 tsp mild taco seasoning
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar or Monterey Jack cheese
  • ½ cup cream cheese or ricotta
  • 1 cup sweet corn
  • ½ cup finely diced red bell pepper
  • ¼ cup milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: chopped parsley for garnish

🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and grease a 9×13-inch baking pan.
  2. Make the biscuit base: Flatten biscuits and press into the bottom and slightly up the sides of the pan. Bake 5–7 minutes until slightly firm.
  3. Cook the meat: Brown ground meat in a skillet. Drain fat and stir in taco seasoning, corn, bell pepper, and milk.
  4. Mix mac n cheese filling: Combine cooked macaroni, shredded cheese, and cream cheese/ricotta until creamy.
  5. Combine filling with meat: Fold cooked meat mixture into mac n cheese filling.
  6. Assemble the bake: Pour filling into pre-baked biscuit base. Sprinkle extra cheese on top if desired.
  7. Bake 20–25 minutes until biscuit base is golden and filling is bubbly.
  8. Serve: Slice and garnish with parsley if desired.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

  • Serve warm, straight from the baking pan
  • Optional: drizzle mild sour cream or cheese sauce
  • Pair with a simple side salad or roasted veggies

🗃️ Storage

  • Store leftovers in fridge (up to 4 days)
  • Freeze slices individually (up to 2 months)
  • Reheat at 325°F (oven) or microwave until hot
